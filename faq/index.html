GAMEPLAY

A survival server with a player-run economy. The difficulty is Hard, PvP is on in the wild, and the market is where most of the game happens: you earn by mining, farming spawners and trading, and spend on land, gear and rank.

Run /rtp to leave spawn, /sethome where you land, then /kit claim for a golden shovel and claim your land. Sell what you gathered with /sell, and open /kit starter for gear, food and two crate keys. /help in game walks you through it.

Mine any spawner without Silk Touch and carry it with you. Up to 64 of the same kind stack into one block. Drops collect inside the spawner instead of on the floor; right-click to open its storage, or put an upgradeable hopper under it. Spawners come from /shop, from ranking up, and from the Spawner Crate.

You lose 0.5% of your balance (0.1%, capped at $100,000, in the dungeon) and drop your inventory, except Chromatic and Halloween gear, which always stays with you. Logging out during a fight costs 5% instead. Keep inventory is on at spawn, in the dungeon and in the KOTH arena.

On in the overworld, the Nether and the End, and in the KOTH arena. Off at spawn, inside claims and in the dungeon. A 15-second combat tag blocks /home, /spawn and teleports while you are fighting.

ECONOMY AND MARKET

$80,000. There is no upkeep and no tax on holding it. The balance leaderboard in the tab list shows where you stand.

Open /ah for four tabs. The auction house holds buy-now listings, up to 75 per player for seven days each. Orders let you post a buy request. Coinflip plays against the house. Liquidity pools are an exchange for elytra, enchanted golden apples, totems, mending books, fireworks, netherite, iron and diamonds, with futures up to 3x leverage. Live prices and history are on the auctions page.

A 5% listing fee with a $100 minimum, and a 2% tax on both the buyer and the seller when it sells.

A second currency used at /craft, where the Chromatic Arsenal, Shards, Clusters, Prisms and other custom items are made. You earn them from crates, kits and the battle pass.

Anything placed in one is sold automatically on a timer, every ten minutes by default and upgradeable down to fifteen seconds, with price multipliers you can level up. Everyone can place two; VIP gets 5, VIP+ 10 and MVP 15.

RANKS

Type /rankup. Each of the ten free ranks, from Tin to Prismatic, asks for things you do in the game: mobs killed, blocks mined, hours played and a balance you have to hold. Only Adamantine takes money from you; every other rank just checks you have it. Each rank hands out spawners, cash or keys.

Convenience, not power: more homes, more Auto Sell Chests, more player warps, extra spawner storage pages, more upgradeable hoppers, commands like /back, /hat, /feed and /repair, faster mcMMO, and a daily kit with crate keys and Prism Shards. VIP, VIP+, MVP and MVP+ each include everything below them. The store lists exactly what each one grants.

Three to start. Silver adds one and Adamantine adds another. VIP has 10, VIP+ 15, MVP 20 and MVP+ 25.

EVENTS AND CRATES

King of the Hill runs every two hours, on the hour, as long as three players are online, in the Backrooms or the Halloween arena. Envoys drop every hour for ten minutes. A vote party fires every twenty votes. The dungeon at /warp dungeon is open all the time. Seasonal events are announced on Discord.

Rewards are in game: the KOTH winner takes a Reward Key, envoys drop spawners and Reward Keys, vote parties give everyone online a Vote Key, and clearing a dungeon camp drops loot worth up to about $5,000,000 at market prices.

Fifty tiers of daily and weekly quests each season. The free track is on for everyone. The Chroma Pass, sold in the store, adds the premium reward on every tier. Season 3, Octoberbit, runs from 1 October to 4 November 2026 and does not carry over from the season before.

Crates sit at spawn and open with a matching key: Chroma, Shard, Spawner and the seasonal Halloween crate from the store, plus Reward and Vote crates earned in game. Each crate also has guaranteed milestone rewards after a set number of opens. Every reward in the store crates is listed on that crate's key in the store, by rarity.

Five sites, each giving a Vote Key every 24 hours: minecraftservers.org, minecraft.buzz, topminecraftservers.org, mc-servers.com and topg.org.

CLAIMS AND PROTECTION

With a golden shovel from /kit claim. Right-click two corners to claim the area; nobody else can build, break or open containers inside. You start with 100 claim blocks and earn 100 more for every hour you play, up to 80,000. Extra blocks come from the store and from crates.

A claim with only a chest in it expires after 7 days and an unused claim after 14. Everything expires after 40 days without logging in, unless you hold at least 40,000 claim blocks or 10,000 bonus blocks.

Explosions and creeper damage are blocked inside claims, and most mob griefing is blocked across the overworld. Raiding unclaimed land is allowed, so claim before you build.

RULES

No cheating or hacking. No griefing. No lag machines. Keep chat civil. Breaking into a claim counts as griefing. Scamming and teleport traps are not banned but are frowned on. Punishments go from mute to temporary ban to ban; the full list is on Discord.
