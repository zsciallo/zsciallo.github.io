Kits are the fixed loadouts you fight with in ChromaPVP duels. Every player gets the same items, so a duel comes down to skill and your own hotbar layout.

Duel kits

Duels use three kits: Sword, Axe and Mace. Each one can be played ranked or unranked. You pick the kit when you queue, from the Play screen (/play) or with /queue and the kit name, such as /queue mace.

  • Sword is diamond gear with a Sharpness sword and nothing else. Hunger and natural regeneration are off, so hits decide the fight.
  • Axe is plain diamond: an axe, a sword, a crossbow, a bow, arrows and a shield. Nothing is enchanted and there is no food. The axe disables shields.
  • Mace is netherite with a Wind Burst mace, wind charges, ender pearls, totems and splash potions. Fall damage is on. Mace duels also get jump pads on the arena floor.

A fourth kit, Chaos, is listed as coming soon and cannot be queued yet.

Layouts and the kit editor

You can move every item of a kit to the slot you want and save it as a layout. Each kit has three free layout slots, so you can keep a few setups and switch between them.

  • /kit shows your kits. Click one to see its layouts.
  • /kit edit with a kit and a slot (for example /kit edit sword 2) opens the editor. Arrange your inventory, then click Save.
  • /kit slot with a kit and a number (for example /kit slot sword 2) picks which saved layout that kit uses.

The editor only opens in the lobby, not while you queue, fight or stand in the FFA pit.

FFA kits

The FFA pit has its own kits. You buy them once with Prism Coins on the Supplies screen, and you lose them when you die. They come in four tabs: Kits, Legendary, Clout and Utility. The strongest armour any of them carries is Protection 3.

Tips

  • Use /practice and a kit name (/practice axe) in the lobby to arm a kit and hit the training dummies. Practice items cannot be thrown or stashed, and /leave puts the kit away.
  • Set your layout before you queue ranked. The default hotbar is rarely the one people end up with.
  • In Axe duels, a raised shield does not save you for long: one axe hit disables it.
