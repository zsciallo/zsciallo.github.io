Ranked is ChromaPVP's rated duel ladder. Each kit has its own rating and tier for the season, and Java and Bedrock players share one ladder.

Getting started

Queue with /queue plus the kit and ranked (for example /queue sword ranked) or from the Play screen (/play). Ranked unlocks at Arena Level 2, which you reach with one win or three losses.

Your first 2 ranked games on a kit each season are placement games. Until you finish them, that kit shows your placement count (such as Placing 1/2) instead of a tier. After the second game you are placed and see your tier.

Divisions

There are seven tiers, from Tin up to Chroma. Every tier except Chroma splits into three divisions, from III at the bottom to I at the top.

Everyone starts a season on the line between Bronze and Silver. Even placements leave you in low Silver, and a bad start drops you into Bronze. Everything above Silver has to be earned.

The top 10 placed players on each kit wear Chroma Elite.

How rating moves

  • A win raises your rating and a loss lowers it. Beating a higher-rated player gains more than beating a lower one.
  • Rating moves most in your first games, when the system knows little about you. It settles as you play more.
  • If you skip a kit for 7 days or more, your next games on it move your rating more than usual.
  • Each kit has its own rating, so a Mace main can start fresh on Sword.
  • You can play the same opponent on the same kit three times a day for rating. After that, your games against them are unrated, and the game says so before it starts.

Your chat tag shows your best tier and division from the kits you have placed on this season. Hover it to see which kit and rating it comes from.

Seasons

Ranked seasons line up with the Battle Pass season. When a new season begins, ratings start fresh and everyone plays placements again. Each season keeps its own leaderboard.

Leaderboards

/leaderboard opens the season's leaderboards, and /leaderboard sword (or any kit name) opens one kit. The screen also shows your own position. The same boards are on the PVP page of this site.

Tips

  • Warm up on the training dummies with /practice before a ranked session.
  • Anyone can watch a ranked game from its first second with /spec, so expect an audience in high tiers.
  • Placements decide where you start, so play them when you are warmed up.
