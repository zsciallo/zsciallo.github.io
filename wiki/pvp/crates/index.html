Obtaining

Cosmetics change how you look in the lobby and in fights. They never change how a fight plays.

Open crates with /crates, or click a crate block in the lobby. Crates only open in the lobby, not in a match or in FFA. Each crate costs Prism Coins:

  • Spectrum Crate, 1,500 Prism Coins: common and rare cosmetics.
  • Mythic Crate, 6,000 Prism Coins: rare and epic cosmetics, plus mythic items you can only get from this crate.
  • Arsenal Crate, 1,250 Prism Coins: one FFA kit.

Buy cosmetics directly in /cosmetics. A preview puts you at the showcase stand in the lobby, where a mannequin wears the item for a few seconds.

The Arsenal Crate

The Arsenal Crate holds every FFA kit from the Supplies screen once, Coin Charms included. Its tiers follow the Supplies tabs:

  • Common: Kits and Utility items priced 800 or less
  • Rare: Kits and Utility items over 800
  • Epic: the Legendary tab
  • Mythic: the Clout tab

The kit goes into your FFA inventory, or into your stash if that is full. If neither has room you get 70% of the kit's price back in Prism Coins. Like any FFA kit, it drops when you die in the pit.

To improve your free FFA gear for good, use the upgrade track on the Supplies screen instead. Its eight steps never drop. See FFA.

x2 Duel

Any crate can be opened as an x2 Duel. You and the server each pull an item from the crate. If yours is worth more you keep both, and if theirs is worth more you get nothing. Ties pull again.

Duplicates and sets

Pulling a cosmetic you already own refunds half its value in Prism Coins.

  • Neon Set: own all ten rares of the Spectrum Crate to get the Neon Glow trail.
  • Mythic Path Set: own all six mythic items of the Mythic Crate to get the Mythic Regalia armour look.

Set rewards cannot drop from a crate. Your progress on each set shows on the crate tiles in /crates.

Cosmetic types

Cosmetics come in four rarities: common, rare, epic and mythic. The types are:

  • Kill effects: particles, a firework or lightning where your opponent falls.
  • Victory effects: a sound, particles and a pose when you win.
  • Death messages: the chat line when you take someone out.
  • Projectile trails: particles behind your arrows, snowballs and other projectiles.
  • Trails: particles at your feet as you move, in the lobby, queues and FFA only.
  • Armour looks: a trim stamped on every armour piece a kit gives you.
  • Titles: a short tag shown with your name.
  • Hats: worn in the lobby only.
  • Auras: a ring that turns around you after you stand still for 5 seconds.

Most cosmetics are sold for Prism Coins. Some come only from crates, and the Champion title only from winning a tournament.

Tips

  • Preview a cosmetic on the showcase stand before you buy it.
  • An x2 Duel can double a pull or lose it, so open normally when you want something certain.
