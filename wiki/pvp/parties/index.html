Parties

Parties let you group up with friends on ChromaPVP and queue team modes together. A party holds up to four players.

Contents

  3. Tips

Commands

  • /party opens your party screen.
  • /party invite and a player's name invites them. Inviting someone starts a party if you are not in one.
  • /party accept [name] and /party deny [name] answer an invite. You can also click [Accept] or [Deny] in chat.
  • /party list shows who is in your party.
  • /party leave leaves the party.
  • /party kick and a name removes a member (leader only).
  • /party disband ends the party (leader only).

An invite runs out after 60 seconds. You can only be in one party at a time.

Queueing together

Only the party leader can queue the party. When the leader queues, every member is moved into the match once it is found.

  • In the Arena, a party of two plays as one team in 2v2 and 2v2v2. In the other formats its players fight apart.
  • A mode refuses a party bigger than its teams.
  • If any member cannot queue right now, or is on another kit server, the party does not queue.
  • Any change to the party (someone joins, leaves or is kicked) takes the party out of the queue. Queue again when you are ready.

Tips

  • Make sure everyone is in the lobby or in FFA, not in a match, before the leader queues.
  • Clicking the invite link in chat is faster than typing /party accept.
