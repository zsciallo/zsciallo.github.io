Daily Streak

Daily Streak

ServerSMP
TypeReward
Command/daily
Ladder7 days
New dayMidnight, New York time

Daily Streak is a reward for every day you log in. Claim it with /daily. The rewards get better for 7 days in a row, then stay at the top.

Contents

  1. Rewards
  2. Mechanics
  3. Tips
  4. History

Rewards

  • Day 1: 1 Vote Key
  • Day 2: 1 Reward Key
  • Day 3: 1 Reward Key and 10 minutes of vote fly time
  • Day 4: 1 Shard Key
  • Day 5: 1 Shard Key
  • Day 6: 1 Spawner Key
  • Day 7: 1 Chroma Key, then again every day your streak lasts

Mechanics

  • You can claim once per calendar day. A new day starts at midnight, New York time.
  • Claim the day after your last claim to move one day up. Miss a day and the streak starts over at day 1.
  • When you join with today's reward unclaimed, the daily screen opens a few seconds later. Press Later to close it; /daily opens it again.

Tips

  • Keys open the crates at spawn. Right-click a crate with its key.
  • Log in for a moment each day to keep the streak, even when you cannot play.
