Crates and Keys are the six reward crates at spawn. Each opens with its own key, and each pays milestone rewards after a set number of openings.

Obtaining

Go to the crates at spawn and right-click a crate to open it with its key. The hologram over each crate shows how many keys you have. Keys are kept on your account, not in your inventory.

  • Chroma Crate (Chroma Key): the full Chromatic and Corrupted gear sets, elytras included, and cash from $500,000 to $2,000,000. Keys come from the store, the Starter Kit, the daily streak, a 14 day vote streak, the monthly top voter awards, the Adamantine rank, the MVP and MVP+ kits, the Reward Crate and milestones.
  • Shard Crate (Shard Key): all 12 shards, plus trackers. Keys come from the store, the Starter Kit, your first vote ever, a 7 day vote streak, 3rd place among monthly top voters, the daily streak, 2nd and 3rd place at KOTH, the VIP and MVP+ kits, the Reward Crate and milestones.
  • Spawner Crate (Crate Key, also called the Spawner Key): every key is a spawner haul. Keys come from the store, the daily streak, the Emerald and Adamantine ranks, the VIP+ kit, the Reward Crate and the Vote Crate milestones.
  • Reward Crate (Reward Key): every crate key except the Vote Key, shards, enchant books (Simple to Legendary) and trackers. Keys come from winning KOTH, envoys and the daily streak.
  • Vote Crate (Vote Key): one key for every vote, plus bonus keys. See Voting.
  • Living Prism Crate (Living Prism Key): the full Living Prism line of armour, tools and weapons, and cash from $500,000 to $5,000,000. The Living Prism Spear, Mace and Wings drop only here. Keys come from the Battle Pass, the store and the crate's own milestone.

The Halloween Crate is retired and can no longer be opened. Halloween gear that players already own still works.

Milestones

Every opening counts toward that crate's milestones. Most ladders start over once you reach the last step; the Vote Crate's pays each step only once.

  • Chroma Crate: 1,500 Prism Shards at 3, 2,000 Prism Shards and a Chroma Key at 5, $1,250,000 at 12, 2 Chroma Keys at 20, a Chromatic Sword at 30, $2,000,000 at 40, a Chromatic Mace at 50.
  • Shard Crate: 1,500 Prism Shards at 3, 2,000 at 5, a Shard Key at 10, 1,500 Prism Shards at 43, 2,000 at 45, a Shard Key at 50, a Shard of Strength at 80.
  • Spawner Crate: 1,500 Prism Shards at 3, 2,000 at 5, a Spawner Key at 50, a Warden spawner at 100.
  • Reward Crate: 750 Prism Shards at 3, 1,250 at 5, a Chroma Key at 15.
  • Living Prism Crate: a Living Prism Key every 8 openings.
  • Vote Crate (once each): a Spawner Crate Key at 2, a Shard Key at 3, a Vote Chestplate at 10, an Elytra at 20, a Chroma Key at 30, a Shard Key at 50.

Tips

  • Keep a free inventory slot. A milestone reward that does not fit drops at your feet.
  • Crafting at /craft is the slow but certain route to a specific Chromatic piece or shard. See Crafting and Upgrades.
