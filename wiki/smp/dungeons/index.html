Obtaining

Clear a camp to open its crate, collect area keys and zone items, take on the mini bosses, and finish at the Rotroot Heart in the core.

Type /dungeon to open the status screen. It shows every camp, its respawn timer, your keys, and four area tiles to warp to:

  • Easy: free. Recommended gear: iron armour.
  • Medium: $10,000. Recommended: Sporewarden or diamond armour.
  • Hard: $20,000. Recommended: Chromatic armour.
  • Very Hard: $40,000. Recommended: Living Prism armour.

/warp heartwood and /warp dungeon take you to the Easy entrance. A warp has a 5 second warm-up: moving, taking damage or dealing damage cancels it. /dungeon leave takes you back to spawn. Plan your route on the dungeon map.

New to Heartwood? /dungeon tutorial walks you through eight steps in Mossgate Landing: kill mobs, craft a Sporewarden piece, beat the mini boss, open a camp crate, upgrade at /craft and finish a contract. It pays 250 Prism Shards and 8 Prismatic Resin. /dungeon tutorial skip skips it.

Areas

The nine main areas, from easiest to hardest, with their mini bosses:

  • Mossgate Landing (Easy): Old Mossback.
  • The Ochre Warrens (Easy): The Kilnmother.
  • Mirewood Roots (Medium): Bogmaw.
  • The Resin Hive (Medium): The Hive Matron.
  • The Pale Drip (Hard): The Pale Matron.
  • The Sulfur Geode (Hard): The Geode Tyrant.
  • Glowberry Canopy (Hard): The Glowmother.
  • The Hollow Below (Very Hard): The Listener.
  • Heartwood Core (Very Hard): the Rotroot Heart, the final boss.

The outskirts are smaller areas with one or two camps each: The Rot Village (Easy), The Drowned Wharf, The Moss Grotto, The Mudflats, Reedwater, River's Bend and The Tidepool (Medium), and Crystal Choir and Spider Hollow (Hard).

If you die in an area, you respawn at the waypoint for its difficulty.

Camps

  • A camp wakes up when a player enters its area. Its mobs hold their ground: pull them too far and they walk back, and they heal when nobody is fighting them.
  • Camp mobs get more health for every extra player fighting them, up to five players.
  • Only kills by players drop anything. Everyone who did at least 10% of a mob's damage and is still near the camp gets their own loot, their own key roll and the kill credit, straight into their inventory.
  • A cleared camp respawns after a few minutes, and sooner when more players are fighting in its area. The Rotroot Heart takes 22.5 minutes.
  • Path guards stand on the walkways that link two areas, sized for the harder of the two. They and the smaller filler camps have no crate: their mobs drop their own loot and keys.

Keys and crates

Each main area has its own key (Mossgate Key, Ochre Key, Mirewood Key, Hive Key, Pale Key, Geode Key, Glowberry Key, Hollow Key and Core Key). A key drops only from that area's mobs and opens only that area's crates. Keys are items: you can trade them, and they stack to 16. /dungeon keys lists yours.

  • A camp's crate unlocks when the camp is cleared, or when its boss dies on a boss camp. It can then be opened 3 times before the camp respawns.
  • Each opening uses one key and gives 5 to 7 different items.
  • Anything that does not fit in your inventory waits in /dungeon claim.
  • Crates hold things like Prismatic Resin, the area's zone armour pieces, spawners, diamonds, resource blocks, Bottles o' Enchanting, golden and enchanted golden apples, random enchant books, a Mending book, StatTrak trackers, ancient debris, money and Prism Shards. The harder crates add Living Prism, and the Hollow Crate and Core Crate can give an elytra.

Bosses

Every mini boss changes at two thirds and one third of its health, usually by hiding behind wardens or crystals you have to break first. With three or more players most of their attacks hit more often or at more targets.

While a boss cannot be hurt, three shields circle it. Kill the guards it called (wardens, drones, crystals or Stalkers) and the shields drop. The Rotroot Heart is shielded below two thirds of its health for as long as a Root Node is alive near it.

  • Old Mossback: a Ground Slam ring, then a knock-up. Hides behind a Moss Ward; break her Moss Wardens.
  • The Kilnmother: Kiln Breath, a fire cone in front of her. Pulls Burrowing Husks out of the walls and hides behind Kiln Wardens.
  • Bogmaw: spits mud pools that poison anyone inside, and its Gulp pulls you in. Hides behind Mire Wardens.
  • The Hive Matron: Resin Rain glues you in place. Kill her drones to reach her, and step aside from her Sting Dash.
  • The Pale Matron: a Stalactite Storm, and her Still Gaze slows you if you keep looking at her. Break her Pale Wardens.
  • The Geode Tyrant: takes no damage until her Geode Crystals are broken. Jump over her Resonance Pulse.
  • The Glowmother: her Flare blinds anyone facing her, and her Vine Cage roots a player until a teammate breaks the vine.
  • The Listener: a Sonic Boom you dodge behind the obsidian pillars, Darkness pulses, and she burrows. Keep moving.
  • The Rotroot Heart: Root Slam in phase one, a Spore Storm in phase two (stand in a Heartlight), and a Heartbeat that hits harder each beat in phase three.

Contracts

A contract summons a private mini boss just for you. Start one with /dungeon contract at a dungeon waypoint or inside an area.

  • Pay Prismatic Resin: 4, 8, 16 or 32 for tiers I to IV (Easy tier I costs 1).
  • Kill 20, 30, 40 or 50 of the zone's mobs. Only your own kills count.
  • Your mini boss then spawns where you stand, with more health at higher tiers. Only you and up to 3 helpers can fight it. Add a helper with /dungeon contract invite and their name before the boss spawns.
  • You have 10 minutes to kill it. If it survives, the contract fails with no refund. /dungeon contract cancel returns your Resin, but only before the boss spawns.
  • Rewards: the zone's item, Prism Shards, Chromabithia XP, the boss's own loot and a contract level. Finishing a tier unlocks the next one in that difficulty.
  • You must do enough of the damage to be paid.

Rotroot Heart

The final boss can drop Rotroot set pieces, the Branch of Heartwood, its trophy, Rotroot Heartseed, the Core Key, Living Prism, Prismatic Resin, spawners, a Blank Spawner and Elite and Unique enchant books.

Rare gear

Heartgrown armour comes only from the Hollow Crate and the Core Crate. The Branch of Heartwood comes from those two crates and the Rotroot Heart. Stats are on Heartgrown and the Branch.

Tips

  • Zone items from a zone's mobs and its contracts pay for that zone's armour set and for Chromatic and Living Prism gear at /craft.
  • Dying in the dungeon costs 0.1% of your balance, at most $100,000. See Keep-on-Death Gear.
  • There is no vote fly in the dungeon.
  • Kills and contracts raise your Chromabithia level, which the Upgrade bench asks for on higher enchant levels.
