Spawners and Hoppers are the SMP's money farm: stacking spawners that store drops and XP, and hoppers that move the drops to a chest.

Obtaining

Spawners here store their drops and XP instead of spawning mobs, and they stack. Upgradeable Hoppers pull those drops out and send them to a chest, ideally an auto-sell chest that pays you while you are offline.

Spawners come from:

  • Mining any spawner you find, for example in a dungeon or mineshaft. You need a stone pickaxe or better, but not Silk Touch, and the spawner goes straight into your inventory.
  • Ranking up: most Explorer ranks give spawners. See Ranks and Kits.
  • The Spawner Crate. See Crates and Keys.
  • Heartwood mobs and dungeon crates. See Dungeons.
  • Crafting at /craft with Blank Spawners. See Crafting and Upgrades.
  • Other players on /ah or through a buy order, and the server market. See Auction House and Market.

Spawners

  • Place a spawner in your claim so nobody can take it. It works while a player is within 128 blocks.
  • Every 20 seconds each spawner in the block produces the drops of 1 to 4 mobs. No mobs actually appear.
  • Right-click it to open its storage and take the drops. You get 1 page of storage (45 slots), and store ranks add more.
  • It also stores up to 100 XP. Take the XP and it mends your gear.
  • Right-click a placed spawner with another of the same mob to stack it, up to 64 in one block. This is how you scale a farm.
  • Sneak and break a spawner to pick it back up.
  • Spawners cannot be blown up.

Upgradeable Hoppers

Craft one in a crafting table: iron, empty, iron on the top row, iron, chest, iron in the middle and empty, iron, empty at the bottom. Place it under a spawner and it pulls 5 stacks out every 3 seconds. Right-click the hopper to open its menu and link it to the chest you want it to fill.

The same menu sells upgrades for money. Each level raises everything at once:

  • Level 1 (free): 1 block suction radius, moves 8 items at a time, 1 link up to 100 blocks away.
  • Level 2 ($14,000): radius 3, 16 items, 2 links up to 200 blocks.
  • Level 3 ($40,000): radius 4, 24 items, 3 links up to 400 blocks.
  • Level 4 ($105,000): radius 5, 32 items, 4 links at any distance.
  • Level 5 ($75,000): 48 items.
  • Level 6 ($200,000): 64 items.
  • Level 7 ($600,000): 128 items.

Chunk hoppers pull loose items in across whole chunks instead of a block radius. They have their own 6 levels, up to a 4 chunk radius and 128 items at a time.

Store ranks raise how many upgradeable hoppers you can have: 150 with VIP, 175 with VIP+, 200 with MVP and 250 with MVP+.

Selling the drops

Spawners do not sell anything themselves. Link a hopper to an auto-sell chest, which sells what is inside on a timer and pays your balance, even while you are logged off. Its timer and a sale multiplier can be upgraded by right-clicking the chest.

  • You can place 2 auto-sell chests, 5 with VIP, 10 with VIP+ and 15 with MVP.
  • The Prismatic rank gives one, and the store sells them.

Or sell by hand with /sell, or list the drops on /ah.

Tips

  • Buy hopper levels early. The transfer amount is what keeps up with a big stack.
  • Collect the stored XP before it reaches 100, or it is wasted.
  • A spare spawner can be salvaged at /craft for Prism Shards and a Blank Spawner, but only one at a time, never a stack.
