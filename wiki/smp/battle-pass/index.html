Battle Pass is the SMP's seasonal reward track. Daily and weekly quests earn points, and points unlock tiers with a reward to claim.

Obtaining

The current season is Season 4, Heartwood.

Everyone has the Free Pass. Open it with /bp (also /battlepass or /pass), and see your quests with /quests. The premium track, the Chroma Pass, is sold on the store and lasts for one season.

Tiers and quests

  • Each tier takes 60 points, so all 50 tiers take 3,000.
  • Rewards are not given automatically. Click a reached tier in /bp to claim it.
  • Daily quests: 7 at a time (3 easy, 2 medium, 1 hard and 1 random), worth 8, 12 or 18 points. They reset every day at 12:00 (Oslo time).
  • Weekly quests: a new week opens every 7 days. Older weeks stay open, so you can catch up. Quests 9 and 10 of each week are for Chroma Pass holders only.
  • Points past tier 50 turn into money: $25 a point on the Free Pass, $75 on the Chroma Pass.

Season 4 has five themed weeks:

  • Week 1, Overgrowth.
  • Week 2, Root and Resin.
  • Week 3, Into the Heartwood: the Easy dungeon areas.
  • Week 4, The Deep Roots: the Medium and Hard areas.
  • Week 5, Heart of the Tree: the boss at the core.

Tips

  • Living Prism Keys open the Living Prism Crate.
  • Weeks 3 to 5 send you into Heartwood, so gear up before then.
  • A new season resets the pass, and a Chroma Pass does not carry over.
