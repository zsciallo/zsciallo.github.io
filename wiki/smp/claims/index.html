Claims protect your land on the SMP: inside one, nobody can break your blocks, open your chests or hurt you.

Obtaining

Only players you trust can build in your claim. Anything outside a claim is fair game.

Take the claim kit with /kit claim for a golden shovel. Right-click a block at one corner of your base, then the opposite corner. The claim covers everything between them, from bedrock to sky. You can also stand in a corner and run /claim. /claimbook gives you the full guide.

  • Your first chest claims a small area around it automatically.
  • A claim must be at least 5 blocks wide and 100 blocks in area.
  • Each claimed block of ground costs one claim block.

Claim blocks

  • You start with 100 claim blocks and earn more every hour you play, up to 80,000 earned this way.
  • Higher Explorer ranks earn them faster. See Ranks and Kits.
  • Claim blocks cannot be bought in game. The store sells bonus claim blocks.
  • /abandonclaim deletes the claim you stand in and gives all its blocks back. Your build is unprotected after that.
  • /claimslist shows every claim you own and how many blocks you have left.
  • Claims never expire.

Trust

  • /trust and a name: they can build and open your chests. Only trust people you know.
  • /containertrust: chests and furnaces only.
  • /accesstrust: doors, buttons and levers only. Nothing else.
  • /untrust takes access away. /trustlist shows who has it in the claim you stand in.
  • /subdivideclaim splits a claim into rooms, so you can trust someone in one part only.

What claims protect

  • Blocks, chests, doors, trapdoors, fence gates, buttons and levers.
  • Your animals, including horses, donkeys and llamas.
  • Players: PvP is on everywhere on the SMP, but nobody can hurt you inside a claim.
  • Explosions do no damage, and fire does not spread or burn.
  • Strangers cannot ender pearl into your claim or set a home there.

Vote Fly works only in claims you own or have build trust in. See Vote Fly.

Tips

  • Claim before you build anything worth keeping. Anything left in unclaimed land is fair game.
  • Breaking into a claimed base is griefing and breaks the rules.
  • Place your spawners inside your claim so nobody can take them.
