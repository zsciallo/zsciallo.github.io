Custom Enchants are enchantments the SMP adds on top of the vanilla ones: 141 of them, for weapons, armour, tools, hoes, fishing rods and two gear sets.

Obtaining

Most custom enchants are bought at the Upgrade bench in /craft. Put in a piece of gear, pick an enchant and buy its next level with Prism Shards and money. The bench sells each enchant up to the max level in the tables below. Levels 3 and up also need a Chromabithia level, which you earn in Heartwood.

  • Random enchant books (Simple, Unique, Elite, Ultimate and Legendary) come from crates and the Battle Pass. Drag a book onto an item in your inventory to apply it.
  • A book can fail. A failed book is used up, but the item is never lost.
  • Chromatic gear comes with its Prism enchants already on it. Halloween gear has its own, from the retired Halloween Crate. The Upgrade bench does not sell those.

Mechanics

  • An item starts with 9 custom enchant slots.
  • Books do not combine. Two books of the same enchant, or a book on an item that already has it, will not raise the level. Levels come from the Upgrade bench and Awakening.
  • Custom enchants that trigger when you hit, shoot or get hit work only against mobs, apart from Backbone, Hardened and the head enchants (Decapitation, Trophy Hunter and Headhunter). Always-on effects such as Overload and Gears still apply.
  • Awakening in /craft destroys one random upgrade on the item and pushes another one or two levels past its cap, up to 10 levels past it. You do not choose which.
  • The Disenchant bench in /craft takes one enchant of your choice off an item for 200 Prism Shards. The enchant is destroyed and nothing is refunded. Thorns and vanilla curses cannot come off.
