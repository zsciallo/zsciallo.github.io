Obtaining

Bonus keys come from voting on every site, keeping a streak and topping the monthly list.

Type /vote in game for the list of sites with clickable links. The five sites and how often each one lets you vote:

  • MinecraftServers.org: every 8 hours.
  • Minecraft.Buzz: every 24 hours.
  • MC-Servers.com: every 24 hours.
  • TopG.org: every 24 hours.
  • TopMinecraftServers.org: every 24 hours.

Rewards

  • Every vote: 1 Vote Key and 2 minutes of Vote Fly.
  • First vote ever: a Shard Key.
  • All 5 sites in one day: a bonus Vote Key.
  • Vote party: when the server reaches 60 votes in a day, everyone online gets a Vote Key. The count starts over each day.
  • Streaks: vote at least once a day. Day 3 gives a bonus Vote Key, day 7 a Shard Key and day 14 a Chroma Key. You can miss one day in seven without losing the streak.
  • Top voters: at the end of each month, 1st place gets 2 Chroma Keys, 2nd a Chroma Key and 3rd a Shard Key.

Vote Keys open the Vote Crate at spawn, which also has its own milestones. See Crates and Keys.

Tips

  • MinecraftServers.org lets you vote three times a day, so it alone is worth 3 Vote Keys and 6 minutes of fly.
  • Vote Fly only works in claims you own or are trusted to build in.
  • Some Battle Pass quests count votes too.
