Obtaining

Explorer ranks run from Tin to Prismatic. The store ranks are VIP, VIP+, MVP and MVP+.

/ranks shows every Explorer rank and what it needs. When you have done everything a rank asks for, run /rankup. Each rank up hands out rewards such as spawners and money, and raises how fast you earn claim blocks.

Store ranks are bought on the store. Each one includes everything in the rank below it.

Explorer ranks

The ladder, in order:

  • Tin (where everyone starts)
  • Bronze
  • Silver
  • Gold
  • Emerald
  • Mithril
  • Orichalcum
  • Adamantine
  • Netherite
  • Prismatic (the final rank, which also gives an auto-sell chest)

Everyone starts with 3 homes. Silver raises that to 4 and Adamantine to 5. Each Explorer rank also shows as a tag after your name in chat.

Store ranks

Each store rank includes everything in the ranks below it, kits too.

VIP: [VIP] chat tag, 10 homes, 5 auto-sell chests, 2 player warps, /hat, /near, /nick, 1.25x mcMMO XP, 2 spawner storage pages, 150 upgradeable hoppers, and the VIP and Diamond kits.

VIP+: [VIP+] tag, 15 homes, 10 auto-sell chests, 3 player warps, /enderchest, /itemname with colour codes, 1.5x mcMMO XP, 3 spawner storage pages, 175 upgradeable hoppers, and the VIP+ kit.

MVP: [MVP] tag, 20 homes, 15 auto-sell chests, 8 player warps, formatting codes in chat, /condense, /feed, /repair and /repair all, /workbench, mcMMO abilities that last 12 seconds longer with halved cooldowns, the mcMMO Lucky perk, 5 spawner storage pages, 200 upgradeable hoppers, and the MVP kit.

MVP+: [MVP+] tag, 25 homes, 250 upgradeable hoppers, and the MVP+ kit.

Without a store rank you have 3 homes, 2 auto-sell chests, 1 player warp, 1 spawner storage page, 125 upgradeable hoppers and /back (also after death).

Kits

/kit lists the kits you can claim. Everyone can claim the Starter, Claim, Tools and Daily kits. Each kit has its own cooldown.

  • Starter Kit: stone tools, chainmail and leather armour, a Claim Shovel, a Claim Finder stick, a bed, 32 bread, a Chroma Key and a Shard Key, plus 30 minutes of Resistance, Saturation and Regeneration. Given once, on your first join.
  • Claim Kit: a Claim Shovel to mark out land. Every minute.
  • Tools Kit: a stone sword, shovel, pickaxe and axe. Every 10 minutes.
  • Daily Kit: a full set of iron armour and iron tools, plus 16 steak. Every 24 hours.
  • Diamond Kit: diamond armour with Protection I, a Sharpness II sword and an Efficiency II pickaxe. Every 24 hours, with VIP and up.
  • VIP Kit: a Shard Key and 200 Prism Shards. Every 24 hours, with VIP and up.
  • VIP+ Kit: a Spawner Key and 500 Prism Shards. Every 24 hours, with VIP+ and up.
  • MVP Kit: a Chroma Key and 750 Prism Shards. Every 24 hours, with MVP and up.
  • MVP+ Kit: a Chroma Key, a Shard Key and 1,000 Prism Shards. Every 24 hours, with MVP+.

Tips

  • Check /ranks early. The first rank up comes quickly, and its spawner is the start of a farm.
  • Rank kits pay Prism Shards every day, which adds up for crafting.
