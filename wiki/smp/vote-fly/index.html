Vote Fly

Vote Fly

ServerSMP
TypePerk
Commands/fly, /fly off, /flytime
Per vote2 minutes
WhereYour claims, or claims you are trusted to build in

Vote Fly is flight time you earn by voting. Each vote banks 2 minutes of /fly, and the bank only counts down while you are in the air.

Contents

  1. Obtaining
  2. Mechanics
  3. Tips
  4. History

Obtaining

Vote for the server with /vote. Every vote adds 2 minutes to your fly bank.

Day 3 of your daily streak adds 10 minutes.

Mechanics

  • Type /fly to take off and /fly off to stop. /flytime shows how much time you have banked.
  • The bank only counts down while you are in the air. Time on the ground is free.
  • A boss bar shows the time left while you fly. It turns yellow at 60 seconds and red at 10.
  • Vote fly works inside a claim you own or have build trust in (/trust). Access or container trust is not enough, and neither is the wilderness.
  • Flying out of the claim drops you with slow falling. Your bank is kept.
  • There is no vote fly in the KOTH arena or the dungeon.
  • A PvP hit grounds you and blocks /fly for 15 seconds.

Tips

  • Vote on every site: each vote banks its own 2 minutes.
