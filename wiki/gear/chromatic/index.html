Chromatic Gear is the SMP's custom armour, tools and weapons. Build it at /craft or win it from the Chroma Crate, and keep it when you die.

Obtaining

Build a piece at the Chromatic bench with /craft, or win it from the Chroma Crate. The mace, spear and elytra have no recipe and only come from the crate. Recipe costs are on Crafting and Upgrades.

  • Armour: helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Wear all four for the set bonus.
  • Weapons: sword, spear, mace, bow and crossbow.
  • Tools: pickaxe, axe, shovel, harvester and angler.
  • The Chromatic Elytra.

Keeping and repairing

Chromatic gear stays with you when you die (see Keep-on-Death Gear). When its durability runs out it does not break. It turns into a Shattered item that keeps its enchants, upgrades, prefix and StatTrak, and /craft repair brings it back.

The armour has gem sockets: see Amethyst Gems.

Tips

  • Upgrade enchants past their starting level at the Upgrade bench in /craft.
  • A Chromatic piece is what you upgrade into its Living Prism twin. Its upgrades, Awakening, prefix and enchants carry over, except Thorns.
  • Salvaging a piece you no longer need gives Prism Shards back.
