Clusters and Prisms fuse shards into one off-hand item. A Cluster joins two shards, and an Apex Prism joins five plus a Cluster.

Obtaining

Fuse them in the Shards section of /craft, or win them from the Shard Crate. Each recipe uses up the shards it is made from.

  • A Cluster gives the effects of its two shards.
  • An Apex Prism gives the effects of five shards and needs a Cluster as well.

Abilities

Hold one in your off hand and right-click to use its ability (a few need sneak and right-click). Each ability has a set number of charges and a cooldown.

Tips

  • Build the Cluster before the Apex Prism that needs it.
  • Clusters and Apex Prisms cannot be salvaged, so the shards inside them are gone for good.
