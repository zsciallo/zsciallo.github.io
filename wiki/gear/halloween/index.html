Halloween Gear is the retired Halloween 2026 line: the Widow armour set and a row of themed weapons and tools. It can no longer be obtained.

Obtaining

Halloween gear can no longer be obtained. It came from the Halloween Crate, which is retired. Gear that players already own still works, and it keeps its enchants and abilities.

  • The Widow set: the Witch Hat, Widow's Carapace, Widow's Legwraps and Widow's Creepers.
  • Weapons such as The Count's Fang, Trick or Stab and The Last Harvest.
  • Tools such as Grave Robber, Grave Digger and Grim Harvester.
  • Coffin Lid, a shield.

Reaper's Toll

Reaper's Toll is a one of one: exactly one exists, and its lore says so. Its Sharpness and Unbreaking are far above anything else on the server.

Tips

  • Halloween gear says It seems bound to you in its lore and stays with you when you die. See Keep-on-Death Gear.
  • It shatters instead of breaking, like Chromatic gear. Repair it with /craft repair.
