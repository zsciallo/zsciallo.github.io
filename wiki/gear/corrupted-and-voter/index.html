Corrupted and Voter Gear are enchanted netherite sets from crates: Corrupted gear from the Chroma Crate and Voter armour from the Vote Crate.

Obtaining

Neither set has a recipe. Both come only from crates.

  • Corrupted gear: armour, the Corrupted Blade, a pickaxe, axe and shovel, and the Corrupted Elytra, from the Chroma Crate.
  • Voter armour: helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots, from the Vote Crate. You get a Vote Key for every vote.

Tips

  • Neither set has a set bonus or a special ability: what you get is the enchants.
  • They are not on the keep-on-death list, which covers Chromatic, Halloween and Living Prism gear. See Keep-on-Death Gear.
