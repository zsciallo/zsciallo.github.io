Trackers add a live counter to a weapon, tool or fishing rod: kills, mob kills, blocks broken or fish caught. Win them from crates.

Contents

  4. History

Obtaining

Trackers come from crates. None of them can be crafted.

  • StatTrak Creator: player kills with a weapon.
  • MobTrak Creator: mob kills with a weapon.
  • BlockTrak Creator: blocks broken with a tool.
  • FishTrak Creator: fish caught with a fishing rod.

Using a tracker

Drag the Creator on top of the item in your inventory to apply it. FishTrak only goes on a fishing rod. The item then shows a counter line in its lore, such as StatTrak Kills or BlockTrak Blocks Broken, and it counts up as you use it.

Tips

  • A shattered Chromatic, Halloween or Living Prism item keeps its StatTrak count.
  • The Living Prism Crate can also give a Sell Chest, an auto-sell chest that sells what is inside on a timer and pays you, even while you are offline. See Spawners and Hoppers.
