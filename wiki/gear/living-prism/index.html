Living Prism Gear is the step above Chromatic: the same armour, tools and weapons, plus wings and the Living Bark set bonus on the armour.

Obtaining

At /craft, each Living Prism recipe upgrades a Chromatic piece into its twin. The Chromatic piece is used up, and its upgrades, Awakening, prefix and enchants carry over, except Thorns. Costs are on Crafting and Upgrades.

The Living Prism Crate gives every piece. Its keys come from the Battle Pass, the store and the crate's own milestone. The spear, mace and wings only come from this crate.

  • Armour: helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots, with Living Bark when you wear all four.
  • Weapons: sword, spear, mace, bow and crossbow.
  • Tools: pickaxe, axe, shovel, harvester and angler.
  • Living Prism Wings.

Keeping and repairing

Living Prism gear stays with you when you die (see Keep-on-Death Gear). At zero durability it shatters instead of breaking and keeps everything on it. Repair it with /craft repair.

Each armour piece has a gem socket: see Amethyst Gems.

Tips

  • Living Prism armour is the recommended gear for the Very Hard areas of Heartwood.
  • If a Chromatic piece holds more gems than its Living Prism twin has sockets, take the extra out before you upgrade it. The bench refuses otherwise.
